THE PET CORNER – MULTAN ONLINE SHOP
=====================================

Included:
- index.html: lightweight mobile-friendly online shop
- 958 catalogue products extracted from your supplied PETS HUT catalogue
- Each listed numeric price is increased by PKR 500
- Search, category filter, cart quantity controls, automatic total
- WhatsApp checkout to 923247908915

IMPORTANT:
This folder is the website. It is not yet a public internet link.
To publish it quickly:
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in your browser.
2. Sign in / create a free Netlify account.
3. Upload or drag this whole folder (the_pet_corner_shop) to the page.
4. Netlify will create a public website URL. Copy that URL and share it with customers.

Orders are sent to your WhatsApp. No customer account or heavy product images are required.
Items whose source catalogue price was blank or "-" are shown as "Price on request" and cannot be added to cart.
